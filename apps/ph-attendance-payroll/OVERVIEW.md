# PH Attendance & Payroll ("Sahod")

An internal HR tool for Philippine companies. HR and admin staff use it to keep employee records, log daily attendance, approve leaves, track cash advances and loans, and run semi-monthly payroll.

Payroll follows Philippine rules: holiday, rest-day, overtime and night-differential premiums, late deductions, employee shares of SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG, BIR withholding tax under the TRAIN law, and automatic loan deductions. Payslips can be printed.

## Sinking Fund
A lending module for a member-funded pool: members contribute capital, which is lent to borrowers at simple monthly interest. Once a loan passes its maturity date unpaid, a fixed month of interest (principal × rate) is added the day after maturity and every month after until fully paid. Tracks loans, payments (with receipts and SMS acknowledgments), overdue accounts and 2% late fees, balance declarations, statements of account, promissory notes and loan agreements, plus a permanent audit trail. All dates follow Philippine time.
